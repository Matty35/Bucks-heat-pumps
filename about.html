The Local Specialists for Off-Gas-Grid Buckinghamshire

We do one thing: design and install heat pumps in the towns and villages of North Bucks — with a bias for the oil and LPG homes the gas grid never reached.

MCS-certified air source heat pump installation for homes in Buckinghamshire, specialising in oil and LPG boiler replacement. Serving Aylesbury, Buckingham, Wendover, Princes Risborough, Winslow and the rural villages of North Buckinghamshire.

Bucks Heat Pumps exists because of a mismatch we kept seeing: the homes with the most to gain from heat pumps — oil-heated villages across the Vale of Aylesbury — were being served either by national installers pricing from a call centre, or by local firms fitting heat pumps as a sideline to boilers. Heat pumps done well are a design discipline, not a plumbing add-on. So that's the company: heat pumps only, surveys before prices, and a patch small enough that the engineer who measured your rooms is the one who commissions your system.

What We Stand Behind

Survey-led, always

No quote leaves this company without a room-by-room heat loss calculation behind it. The design SCOP is on your paperwork — a number we're accountable to, not a brochure claim.

Fixed means fixed

Your quote itemises radiators, cylinder, electrics and making-good, with the grant deducted. If we missed something at the survey, that's our cost, not yours.

Local and staying local

Based in Aylesbury, working Aylesbury to Buckingham and the villages between. When you call, an engineer answers — and the same van does your annual service.

How We Work

The pattern is on every page of this site because it's the whole method: a free heat loss survey, an honest conversation about whether your house is ready (and occasionally, that a £400 loft top-up should come first), a fixed quote with the grant deducted, a two-to-three-day installation, and a system handed over with the controls set for your house and your tariff. For oil and LPG homes we also sequence the £9,000 grant window so your application lands the day vouchers open. If we don't think a heat pump is right for your property, we'll tell you at the survey — a bad install would live twenty minutes from our office, and village word travels.
